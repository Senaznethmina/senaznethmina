01 / About

Two disciplines,
one process.

I started in packaging and print — where a design has to survive being printed, cut, folded, and held. That same discipline now shapes how I build for screens: systems that hold up, not mockups that just look good in a deck.

Alongside client and studio work, I study Business Studies, Accounting, and Computer Science at the A/L level — a fairly literal way to end up doing brand strategy and code in the same afternoon.
